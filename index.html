Settings and assumptions

Railcar gallons start at a typical 19,700. For a car that differs, type its gallons from the railcar sheet. The unload time is gallons divided by the GPM. The tank size is worked out from how much a 5,000 gal load of 36% acid drops the sonar, so change that number if you see a different drop. Other products draw less in proportion to the 36% acid in them.

This tool suggests an order. It assumes every available loader is free right now. While a railcar unloads, a loader is only tied up for rig up, then for venting and rig down when it finishes. You make the call.
